FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Have Questions? We’ve Got Answers

Find quick answers to common questions about our groundwater exploration, water resource management and environmental solutions.

01 |

What is a geophysical survey and how does it help?

A geophysical survey is a scientific method used to identify the presence, depth and potential of groundwater by studying subsurface geological formations. It helps in selecting the most suitable location for drilling. reducing risk and improving success rates.

02 |

What types of wells do you construct?

We construct high-yield production tube wells, deep agricultural borewells, commercial open wells, artificial recharge wells, and customized industrial water well systems using advanced rotary and DTH drilling rigs.

03 |

Do you provide solutions for open wells as well?

Yes, we offer complete assessment, desilting, deepening, radial bore extension, and concrete ring lining solutions to maximize open well storage and recovery rate.

04 |

What is a recharge well and why is it important?

A recharge well captures and filters rainwater or surface runoff, percolating it into deep underground aquifers to replenish depleted water tables and ensure sustainable water security.

05 |

Do you offer CGWA consultancy services?

Yes, we provide end-to-end CGWA permissions, comprehensive hydrogeological impact assessment reports, piezometer installations, and statutory regulatory compliance.

06 |

In which industries do you provide your services?

We serve manufacturing plants, chemical & pharmaceutical industries, commercial infrastructure, agricultural projects, townships, and institutional campuses.
